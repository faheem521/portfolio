01 Live product Multi-tenant SaaS Full-stack

Ordexa — Commerce operations platform for COD merchants

A SaaS that runs the full post-order journey for cash-on-delivery sellers in Pakistan and South Asia: confirmation, courier booking, tracking, returns, cash collection and customer retention.

Role
Primary full-stack engineer
Timeline
2026 — present
Team
2 engineers; ~85% of commits
Platforms
Web app, Shopify app, marketing site

The problem

COD merchants lose margin to fake orders, failed deliveries and manual cash reconciliation. They also track shipments across several courier portals and juggle disconnected tools for orders, inventory and customer messaging.

What I built

  • A multi-tenant Laravel API and React/TypeScript SPA with organization-scoped data, per-channel access and custom roles with per-member permission overrides.
  • A courier integration layer with a common driver interface over 7 Pakistani couriers (TCS, Leopards, PostEx, M&P, Daewoo, Tranzo, Flaship) for booking, tracking, load sheets and city-code sync.
  • Sales channel sync for Shopify and Salla (Saudi Arabia) via OAuth, webhooks and order import, and Ordexa ships as an embedded Shopify app.
  • COD finance tools: receivables aging, courier remittance batches, bank-statement matching and remittance disputes.
  • A reporting engine with nightly rollups, integrity checks, and saved, scheduled and exportable reports.
  • Order confirmation and retention: a WhatsApp Cloud API inbox and campaigns, a Node.js WhatsApp gateway, voice-call confirmation, order risk scoring and AI-assisted address correction.
  • Subscription billing with plans, add-ons and credit wallets across Stripe, Shopify Billing and manual payments, plus a super-admin console.

Architecture & engineering notes

Multi-tenancy & access

  • A global organization scope on the base model isolates tenant data by default, and a channel scope limits non-owners to the sales channels assigned to them.
  • Team-scoped roles and permissions (Spatie), an owner bypass, 2FA and super-admin impersonation for support.
  • Plan limits are enforced in the database by a usage guard, with Redis counters synced on a schedule.

Background processing

  • Queued jobs on Redis/Horizon handle Shopify sync, imports and exports, WhatsApp messaging and courier tracking.
  • Scheduled jobs run courier tracking pulls, COD aging alerts, nightly report rollups, recurring expenses and template syncs.
  • Webhook handlers receive events from Shopify, Salla, Stripe, WhatsApp and the voice provider.

Marketing site

  • Built ordexa.cc in Next.js 15 as an SEO-focused static export. Blog, pricing, integrations and FAQs are pulled from the platform API at build time.
  • A token-protected rebuild webhook regenerates the site whenever content changes in the admin.

Quality

  • Pest test suite, PHPStan/Larastan static analysis and Pint formatting on the back end, and strict TypeScript with Zod-validated forms on the front end.
  • Order items snapshot product data at the time of purchase, so historical orders and reports stay accurate when the catalog changes.
02 Live on Shopify App Store Shopify app Full-stack

Fundlify — Crowdfunding & pre-orders for Shopify

A public Shopify app that lets merchants run Kickstarter-style crowdfunding, pre-order and pre-launch campaigns inside their own store.

Role
Full-stack Shopify engineer
Timeline
2025 — 2026
Team
3 contributors; 230+ of my commits
Platforms
Embedded admin app + storefront widget

The problem

Merchants who want to validate demand or fund a production run usually send customers to an external crowdfunding platform. That loses the customer relationship, the checkout and the data. Fundlify keeps the whole campaign inside Shopify.

What I built

  • The campaign builder (create and edit flows, rewards and tiers, pre-order and pre-launch phases) on Remix with Polaris and App Bridge.
  • The storefront campaign widget in Vue 3, Pinia and PrimeVue, shipped as a theme app extension and served through an app proxy.
  • A merchant notification system with editable email templates for reservations and each pre-order stage, sent via Resend and React Email.
  • Billing plans and usage-based charges through the Shopify Billing API, plus a merchant setup guide and dashboard.
  • Checkout and order handling via the GraphQL Admin API: selling plans, draft orders, discount codes and inventory updates. I also shipped the add-ons feature to production.

03 Live marketplace Multi-vendor B2B Shopify + Laravel

Sourcequip — Multi-vendor B2B equipment marketplace

A marketplace built on Shopify where independent sellers list equipment, manage orders and get paid, while buyers send inquiries and RFQs directly to sellers.

Role
Full-stack engineer, seller & admin portals
Timeline
2025 — 2026
Team
4–5 engineers; ~190 of my commits
Languages
English & Chinese

The problem

Shopify is designed around a single seller. The client needed many vendors on one storefront, each with their own catalog, orders, commissions, payouts and membership tier. Admins also needed approval and moderation tools, and sellers needed a portal in their own language.

What I built

  • Large parts of the seller and admin portals in React and Polaris on a Laravel 12 back end: product listings, add and edit product flows synced to Shopify, and category management.
  • The seller onboarding wizard, seller profiles and seller listings, with an admin approval flow that includes one-click approval from email.
  • Seller-facing orders, earnings and payouts views on top of the platform's commission engine.
  • Full English and Chinese localization across the portals and the registration flow.

Platform capabilities

Global and per-seller commissions, Stripe membership tiers (Laravel Cashier), buyer inquiries and RFQs via a Shopify app proxy, seller profiles synced to Shopify metaobjects, webhook-driven catalog and order sync, and a custom Online Store 2.0 theme with seller storefront, RFQ and product-compare sections.
